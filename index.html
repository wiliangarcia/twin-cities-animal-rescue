Welcome to Twin Cities Animal Rescue

We are a dedicated non-profit organization focused on rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need throughout the Twin Cities area.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give every animal a second chance at a loving, permanent home while promoting compassionate pet care, community education, and accessible foster opportunities.

Our Impact in Numbers

  • 1,200+ Rescue animals placed into safe foster homes last year
  • 950+ Successful permanent adoptions facilitated
  • 350+ Active volunteers supporting daily operations

How You Can Help

There are many ways to support our mission: adopt a pet, open your home to a temporary foster animal, donate critical supplies, or volunteer your time.
